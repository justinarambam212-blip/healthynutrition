Privacy & Security

Your privacy and safety are important to us.

🛡️

Your Privacy Matters

At HealthyNutrition, we respect your privacy and aim to keep your personal information safe. This page explains what information we collect, why we use it, and how you can manage your information.

👤

Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

When you create and use your HealthyNutrition account, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Blood group
  • Height and weight
  • Profile photo, if you choose to add one
  • Dietary preferences and food allergies
💚

How We Use Your Information

Why we need your information

Your information may be used to provide and improve HealthyNutrition services, including:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Personalizing nutrition recommendations
  • Calculating nutrition requirements
  • Providing relevant food and diet information
  • Improving your experience with the application
  • Communicating important account information
🔐

Data Security

Keeping your information protected

We aim to protect your personal information using appropriate security measures. Access to personal information should be limited to authorized systems and individuals who need it to provide the service.

🔒 Never share your password or account credentials with anyone.
⚙️

Manage Your Information

You are in control of your profile

You can review and update your personal information through the Settings page.

⚙️ Go to Settings
🧒

Children's Privacy

Keeping younger users safe

HealthyNutrition is designed to provide a simple and friendly experience for users of different age groups. For younger users, parents or guardians should help manage personal information and account settings.

🍪

Cookies & Local Storage

How your browser may store information

HealthyNutrition may use browser storage or similar technologies to remember certain preferences and improve the user experience.

🗑️

Your Data

Managing or removing your information

If you want to update, correct, or request deletion of your account information, you can contact the HealthyNutrition support team.
